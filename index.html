🇴🇲 Oman — Muscat

Stopover on 23 Dec 2026 and overnight on 4–5 Jan 2027. Estimated total for Oman (activities, food, local transport, excl. hotels and options): EUR 455.

Entry: visa-free for up to 14 days (Italian and Brazilian passports). Carry a passport valid 6+ months, the onward ticket, a hotel booking and travel insurance that covers Oman.

Dress: Grand Mosque — women in loose clothes covering arms and legs plus a headscarf; men in long trousers. Elsewhere: modest clothing, no beachwear in town.

Getting around: Otaxi app or the official airport taxi desk. Cards accepted almost everywhere. Weather: about 25°C, sunny.

🇹🇭 Phuket

24–31 Dec 2026 and the day of 4 Jan 2027. Base: Benyada Lodge, Surin Beach — all tours below pick up at the hotel unless noted. Estimated total for Phuket (activities, food, local transport, excl. hotels and options): EUR 1215.

Entry: fill in the TDAC digital arrival card at tdac.immigration.go.th within 3 days before arrival (both of you).

Getting around: Grab or Bolt apps. From Surin: Phuket Town 35 min, Big Buddha / Promthep 45–60 min, airport 30 min. Weather: about 31°C, sea 28°C, dry season.
