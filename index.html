🇴🇲 Oman — Muscat

0%

Stopover on 23 Dec 2026 and overnight on 4–5 Jan 2027. Estimated total for Oman ≈ EUR 290 (activities, food, local transport, excl. hotels — adjusted after removing the sunset dhow cruise and Bait Al Luban dinner from the original EUR 455 plan).

Entry: visa-free up to 14 days (Italian and Brazilian passports). Carry a passport valid 6+ months, the onward ticket, a hotel booking and travel insurance that covers Oman.

Dress: Grand Mosque — women in loose clothes covering arms and legs plus a headscarf; men in long trousers. Elsewhere: modest clothing, no beachwear in town.

Getting around: Otaxi app or the official airport taxi desk. Cards accepted almost everywhere. Weather: about 25°C, sunny.
